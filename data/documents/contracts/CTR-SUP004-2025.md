# Secondary Supply Framework Agreement: Silicon Craft Technologies (SUP-004)

### Section 1: Pre-Qualified Alternate Supplier Status
Silicon Craft Technologies is a certified ISO-9001 and IATF-16949 secondary supplier for Microcontroller and Processing ICs (SKU-1042, SKU-1001, SKU-1002). All production lines at Bengaluru facility are pre-qualified for direct delivery to Chennai (SITE-CHN-01) and Bengaluru (SITE-BLR-02) plants without re-qualification inspection.

### Section 2: Lead Times and MOQ
- SKU-1042: Contract price INR 1,520 per unit. Standard lead time is 5 business days.
- SKU-1001: Contract price INR 680 per unit. Standard lead time is 6 business days.
- SKU-1002: Contract price INR 295 per unit. Standard lead time is 5 business days.
- MOQ: 300 units. Emergency buffer capacity reserved: up to 3,000 units per calendar month.

### Section 3: Priority Dispatch & Emergency Surcharges
- Expedited 3-day turnaround is guaranteed upon payment of an emergency handling surcharge of INR 25,000 per order.
- Freight is coordinated via dedicated express road freight (LANE-BLR-CHN-RD) or air cargo.

### Section 4: Mutual Non-Exclusivity
ResilienceHub is free to place secondary and surge demand purchase orders without minimum annual commitment quotas, provided orders meet MOQ thresholds.

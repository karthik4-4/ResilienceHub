# Master Supply Agreement: Apex Micro Systems (SUP-001)

### Section 1: Scope & Products
Apex Micro Systems is designated as the Primary Tier-1 Supplier for Industrial Microcontroller Units (SKU-1042, SKU-1001) to ResilienceHub manufacturing facilities including the Chennai Plant (SITE-CHN-01) and Bengaluru Plant (SITE-BLR-02).

### Section 2: Pricing and Standard Lead Times
- SKU-1042: Base Contract Price INR 1,450 per unit. Standard delivery lead time is 12 business days from purchase order confirmation.
- SKU-1001: Base Contract Price INR 650 per unit. Standard delivery lead time is 10 business days.
- Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): 500 units per order.

### Section 3: Force Majeure & Disruption Protocols
In the event of manufacturing line stoppages, component shortages, or wafer fabrication delays exceeding 7 calendar days:
- The Supplier must provide written notification within 24 hours of identifying the delay.
- ResilienceHub reserves the right to source from qualified alternate suppliers without contractual penalty.
- Late delivery penalty: 0.75% of purchase order value per business day of unexcused delay, capped at 15%.

### Section 4: Expedited Production
Expedited batch delivery can reduce lead time to 7 business days subject to a standard expedite fee of INR 35,000 plus 10% unit price surcharge. Maximum monthly expedited allocation is 2,500 units.

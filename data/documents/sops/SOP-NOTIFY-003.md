# SOP: Disruption Stakeholder Notification Matrix

### 1. Mandatory Recipients by Action
- **REORDER Action**: Procurement Manager for the destination plant and Supplier Account Representative must always be alerted immediately via Email.
- **REROUTE Action**: Logistics Manager and Carrier Dispatcher must be dispatched route shift orders.
- **HOLD Action**: Supply Chain Planner must receive log for scheduled review.

### 2. Escalation Thresholds
- High Financial Impact: If total mitigation expenditure exceeds INR 1,500,000, Finance Controller must be included in notifications.
- Critical Stockout Warning: If days of cover is under 3 days, Plant Manager must receive immediate SMS broadcast.
- Unmitigated Disruption: If no viable mitigation exists (Action=HOLD with escalation), Escalation Director and VP Operations must be notified via urgent SMS and high-priority email.

# Freight Advisory: Chennai Port Terminal Congestion & Customs Delays

### 1. Incident Overview
Deep depression in the Bay of Bengal and seasonal container congestion at Chennai Port Terminal have caused average berthing delays of 5 to 7 days for incoming container feeder vessels from Singapore and East Asia (`LANE-SIN-CHN-SE`).

### 2. Bypass and Contingency Routes
- Air freight bypass via Singapore Airlines Cargo directly to Chennai Airport (`LANE-SIN-CHN-AR`) operates on daily scheduled flights with 48-hour clearance.
- Domestic inter-plant transfers from Bengaluru (`LANE-BLR-CHN-RD`) or Pune (`LANE-PUN-BLR-RD`) remain unaffected.

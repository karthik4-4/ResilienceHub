# Freight Advisory: Mumbai-Pune Expressway Blockage & Rail Bypass

### 1. Incident Overview
Heavy monsoon rainfall has caused rockslides across the Khandala Ghat stretch of the Mumbai-Pune Expressway. Heavy commercial vehicle movement along road corridor `LANE-MUM-PUN-RD` is halted for an estimated 48 to 72 hours.

### 2. Available Alternative Corridors
- Central Railway Freight Corridor (`LANE-MUM-PUN-RL`) remains 100% operational with green-corridor priority for industrial container rakes.
- Added transit delay via rail is +1 to +2 days compared to standard road transit.
- Rail capacity is ample (up to 35,000 units per rake), with low risk index.
